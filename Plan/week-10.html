Hour 1: Algorithms in Python

  • Solve LeetCode #380: Insert Delete GetRandom O(1).
  • Redo LeetCode #146: LRU Cache in a blank editor, without looking at your old solution.
  • Rule: try each problem on your own for at most 25 minutes. If you are stuck, watch the NeetCode video for that problem, close it, and then write the solution again from memory in Python.
10:40 – 11:40 a.m.

Hour 2: Learn: Java concurrency basics

  • Learn threads, ExecutorService, CompletableFuture, the synchronized keyword, and the classes AtomicInteger and ConcurrentHashMap.
  • Learn what a race condition is and what a deadlock is.
11:50 a.m. – 12:50 p.m.

Hour 3: Exercises: concurrency

  • Exercise 1: start 10 threads that each add 1,000 to a shared int counter, and see that the result is wrong. Fix it first with synchronized, then with AtomicInteger.
  • Exercise 2: use an ExecutorService with 4 threads to process a list of 20 tasks, then shut it down properly.
  • Exercise 3: use CompletableFuture to run three slow methods at the same time and combine their results.
  • Commit and push.
5:30 – 6:30 p.m. (after work)

Hour 4: Job search

  • Applications (35 minutes): send 3 applications to Java backend or software developer roles from your tracker. For each one, adjust the top of your resume so it uses 2 or 3 keywords from that job description.
  • Networking (10 minutes): send 2 short LinkedIn messages using the template on the Resources page. Send them to former colleagues from Capgemini, Deloitte or Sopra Steria, or to developers at one of your target companies.
  • Interview practice (15 minutes): Practise answering “What are your salary expectations?” Research the typical range for your target role and city first.
  • Before you stop, write every application and message you sent today into your tracker.
