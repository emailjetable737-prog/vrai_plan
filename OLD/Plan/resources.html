For former colleagues, start with how you know each other. Keep every message short and polite, and never send the same person more than one follow-up.

STAR story template

Use this structure for each of your six stories. Each story should take less than two minutes to say out loud.

  1. Situation: where you were and what the context was, in one or two sentences.
  2. Task: what you were responsible for.
  3. Action: what you did, step by step. This is the longest part. Say “I”, not “we”.
  4. Result: what happened in the end, and what you learned.

Your six stories: (1) a hard technical problem you solved, (2) a disagreement with a teammate or manager, (3) a mistake and what you learned, (4) learning something new very quickly, (5) “Tell me about yourself”, and (6) why you are moving from QA into development.

You do not need to talk about your leave in interviews. You are still employed at Cognizant, so there is no gap on your resume.

Your project at the end of the plan

Order Manager is a Spring Boot REST API for customers, products and orders, using PostgreSQL. When an order is created, it publishes an event to Kafka, and a second service called notification-service consumes it safely. It is secured with Spring Security and JWT and uses a Redis cache. The whole system runs with Docker Compose, on Kubernetes, and on AWS, it is tested with JUnit, Mockito and Testcontainers, and every push runs through GitHub Actions.
